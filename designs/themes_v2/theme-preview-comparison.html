🎯 Design for Price Comparison

Each color serves a specific purpose in helping users save money

🔵
Trust Blue

Primary color for CTAs, links, and navigation. Builds confidence in purchase decisions.

💚
Savings Green

THE MOST IMPORTANT. Highlights best prices, savings amounts, and deals. Universally recognized for money-saving.

🟠
Urgency Orange

Hot deals, limited time offers, and deal of the day. Creates sense of urgency to act.

🟡
Featured Amber

Premium store placements, featured listings. Used for Phase 2 monetization.

⚪
Clean Neutrals

Background and text colors. Ensures prices and specs are easily scannable in tables.

🔴
Alert Red

Out of stock, errors, price increases. Negative states that require attention.

💡 Design Principles

💰 Savings-First

Green highlights best deals. Users instantly see where to save money.

👀 Scannable

Clear price hierarchy. Tabular numbers. Easy comparison tables.

✅ Trustworthy

Professional blue builds confidence. Users trust price accuracy.

⚡ Action-Driven

Clear CTAs. Urgency badges. Direct paths to stores.

📊 Data-Focused

Clean tables. Consistent spacing. Perfect for comparing specs.

🌍 Localized

Optimized for Arabic RTL and English LTR with proper fonts.
