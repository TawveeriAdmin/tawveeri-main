# TAWVEERI — LAUNCH & MARKETING PLAYBOOK
**Built from direct research into Saudi X, local retail copy, and product-list UX**
**Companion to `docs/LAUNCH_VOCABULARY.md` — that file governs claims; this one governs
voice, order and format.**

---

## 0. THE ONE RULE ABOVE ALL

Every phrase used publicly must exist in `LAUNCH_VOCABULARY.md` under CAN SAY.
If a phrase is not there, it does not get used — it gets proposed, with evidence, and the
vocabulary is amended first.

**This document never overrides that. It only decides how the permitted words are
arranged.**

## 1. WHAT THE RESEARCH FOUND

### X in Saudi Arabia is a majlis, not a billboard
X is the strongest platform in the Kingdom for shaping public opinion and direct
engagement, and it reflects the level of conversation in the society itself. People use
it to discover products and discuss recommendations during shopping seasons.

**Implication:** a launch written as a press release will not be read. A launch written as
one person telling people something useful will be.

### The Saudi shopper is already sceptical of discounts
Complaints about inflated "before" prices and misleading coupons are a running theme on
local social platforms. The Ministry of Commerce requires that the price before and after
a discount be shown to the consumer.

**Implication:** our finding is not an accusation — it is a measurement against a standard
that already exists. Frame it that way.

### Local retail copy leads with benefit, in one short line
noon uses conversational framing. Extra uses direct imperatives — "تسوّق", "قسّمها على
راحتك". Jarir is more formal, and leads on trust elements: genuine products, warranty,
delivery.

**Implication:** take their directness and speed. Do not take the exaggeration, the
countdown timers, the FOMO, or "لفترة محدودة" — our own stock signal was measured
unreliable, and those patterns are what we exist to oppose.

### The results list is where a shopper is won or lost
Baymard reports abandonment of 67–90% on mediocre product lists, versus 17–33% on
slightly optimised ones. 78% of mobile sites score poor to mediocre. Tawveeri is
mobile-first.

**Implication:** the product page matters less than the list that leads to it. Verify these
figures independently before citing them anywhere public.

### Explanation builds trust; personality does not
Peer-reviewed work is consistent: explainability strengthens consumer trust, and trust is
the strongest predictor of AI-assistant adoption. In higher-stakes contexts, human-like
cues must support clear explanation rather than overshadow it.

**Implication:** وفّر explains why it recommended something. It does not become a mascot.
**Saving is the hero of the page — وفّر assists.**

## 2. THE VOICE

**Light Saudi in headlines and calls to action. Simple MSA in explanation and evidence.**

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| «قبل تشتري، شوف فرق السعر» | «وش السالفة مع الأسعار؟» |
| «دوّر على منتجك» | «تهدف المنصة إلى تمكين المستهلك» |
| «شوف من أي متجر جا السعر» | «نسعى لتقديم تجربة تسوق متكاملة» |
| «وإذا ما عرفنا، نقولها بوضوح» | أي صياغة مؤسسية طويلة |

Not heavy dialect — that makes a comparison platform read like an entertainment account.
Not corporate formal — that reads like nobody wrote it.

---

## 3. THE ORDER OF THE MESSAGE

**Benefit → how it works → the evidence → the limits.**

Not: philosophy → methodology → benefit.

A first-time visitor does not yet know what Tawveeri is. The honesty is what makes them
stay; it is not what makes them look.

> **«الأقوى فينا ليس تغطيتنا — بل أننا نقول للناس ما لا نعرفه»** is the **closing** of the
> story, never the opening.


## 5. LONGER FORM — website, LinkedIn, press

```
قبل تشتري، شوف فرق السعر.

أطلقنا توفيري لمساعدة المستهلك في السعودية على البحث عن المنتجات
ومقارنة الأسعار المتاحة بين المتاجر بطريقة أوضح.

في توفيري نبيّن لك من أي متجر جاء السعر، ومتى رصدناه، وعندما تختار
العرض ننقلك إلى صفحة المنتج نفسه في المتجر.

وننظر إلى الخصومات بطريقة مختلفة: لا نعتمد على السعر المشطوب وحده،
بل نحسب التوفير من الأسعار التي رصدناها فعلًا، متى كانت البيانات كافية.

توفيري ما زال في بدايته، لذلك لن ندّعي أن تغطيتنا كاملة أو أن كل
سعر محدَّث لحظيًا. ما نعرفه نعرضه، وما لا نعرفه نقوله بوضوح.

هذه هي النسخة الأولى، وستتحسن التغطية والتجربة مع كل مرحلة.
```

## 6. VIDEO — Instagram and TikTok, 15–20 seconds

Vertical. Motion, not stills. Screen recording of the real product, not a founder talking
head.

```
0–2s   قبل تشتري… شوف فرق السعر.
2–6s   [تسجيل شاشة] كتابة اسم منتج في توفيري
6–12s  [النتائج] السعر · المتجر · وقت الرصد
12–16s [الانتقال] صفحة المنتج نفسها في المتجر
16–20s وإذا ما عرفنا، نقولها بوضوح. جرّب توفيري.
```

Hook in the first two seconds. Problem then solution. The product is the subject of the
frame. One clear call to action at the end.

## 7. NEVER USE IN PUBLIC COPY

- **Any hardcoded retailer count.** Removed from the site for this reason, and now a
  standing rule in the vocabulary
- **Any catalogue-size figure** — 5,398, ~9,300, or any successor. They count inventory,
  not what a customer can compare
- **Internal engineering figures** — 112/112, 770/770, 1,283 tests, harness results. They
  are evidence for us, not a benefit to a customer, and quoting them invites a question we
  have no interest in answering
- **"حصرية" on coupons** unless a specific coupon is contractually exclusive to Tawveeri
- **Any cadence** — "يوميًا", "باستمرار", "لحظي", "محدَّث". Freshness currently comes from
  discovery, which is coverage, not a guaranteed refresh schedule
- **Any comprehensive-market claim** — "كل الأسعار في السعودية"
- **LuLu or Sharaf DG named as comparison sources**
- **Any partnership claim.** The Amazon affiliate programme is the only one, and noon's
  publisher account is not a partnership

## 8. THE NUMBERS — check before every use

The single publishable figure is defined in `LAUNCH_VOCABULARY.md` §10: **comparable
products across retailers a customer can actually see.**

It has moved repeatedly — 419 → 705 → 732 → 740 — so **re-measure before any public use**
and never carry a figure forward from a document.

The successor rule holds: **a new number never inherits the old number's sentence.** If it
counts a different population, it gets its own line.

## 9. THE FRAME, IN ONE LINE

```
عربي:      نُظهر لك السعر، ومن أين جاء، ومتى رصدناه — وحين لا نعرف، نقول ذلك.
English:   We show you the price, where it came from, and when we observed it —
           and when we don't know, we say so.
```

## 10. THE IDENTITY

Tawveeri's positioning is not "the biggest" and not "always the cheapest."

It is: **we help you see the price difference, and we show you the evidence we built it
on.**

Everything in this playbook exists to make that legible to a Saudi shopper in the seconds
they will give it.
