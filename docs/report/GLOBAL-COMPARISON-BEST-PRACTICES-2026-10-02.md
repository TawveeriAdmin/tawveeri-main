# Global best practices for a decision-grade price-comparison platform

Research brief for Tawveeri engineering (Arabic-first, electronics/appliances, no checkout, merchant hand-off via `/go`, own price-observation history). Date: 2026-10-02. Web research only; no code was read or changed.

Tagging: **[verified: URL]** = claim read on that page (or in a search snippet quoting it). **[unverified]** = could not find a citable source; treat as a hypothesis, not a fact. Where the only source is a third-party blog rather than the platform itself, the tag says "secondary".

---

## 1. idealo (DE) — identity, total price, placement, history

- **Identity by EAN/GTIN/MPN.** Merchant feeds require SKU, brand, name, URL, price incl. VAT, shipping cost, delivery time, image and "identifiers such as EAN/GTIN or MPN"; "EAN/GTIN is absolutely essential — without it the product won't be matched to the catalog and ends up in the 'free catalog' where visibility is significantly lower." Matching is "fuzzy logic and AI automated matching" plus human QC. [verified, secondary: https://www.mergado.com/blog/successful-sale-on-idealo, https://rextheme.com/list-woocommerce-products-on-idealo-feed/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idealo]
- **Shipping is a first-class feed field**, itemised per payment method (or 0.00 if uniform), explicitly so shoppers can compare total cost and avoid checkout surprises. [verified, secondary: https://www.mergado.com/blog/successful-sale-on-idealo]
- **Sort default vs. "cheapest total" highlight.** Default list order is by item price (lowest first); idealo *additionally highlights* the offer with the lowest total incl. shipping, so "a low item price with high shipping can lose this highlight to a pricier offer with free shipping." Users can switch to sort by total price. [verified, secondary: https://pricepirate.com/docs/en/solutions/idealo-repricing, https://priceva.com/de/blog/idealo-preisvergleich, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idealo]
- **Offers without a shipping cost:** how idealo ranks them is **[unverified]** (German law requires price-search listings to state concrete shipping costs, which is why the feed field is mandatory — https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/preisangabenverordnung.html).
- **Paid placement.** "Advertising does not influence organic ranking within its price comparison. Sponsored placements are clearly labelled and kept separate from the organic comparison results." [verified, secondary: https://www.productsup.com/blog/idealo-product-feed/] Tie-breaking: reportedly offers with the (now discontinued) idealo Direktkauf ranked first on equal price, then price incl. shipping. [unverified — only in search snippets; Direktkauf ended 2022 per https://www.loewenstark.com/wissen/idealo-auch-ohne-direktkauf-option-erfolgreicher-marktplatz/]
- **Preisentwicklung + Preiswecker UX.** A "Preisentwicklung" box on the product page shows the price curve for the last 3/6/12 months (90 days by default); the alert is set *from inside the chart* ("Preiswecker") with a desired price **incl. shipping** and email; email fires when any merchant reaches it. [verified, secondary: https://www.shoppingvorteil.de/spartipp-idealo-preisvergleich/, https://www.giga.de/ratgeber/tipps/idealo-preisvergleich-mehr-sparen-mit-allen-funktionen-der-seite/]

## 2. Kakaku.com (JP) — history curve, timing, offer table

- **Price-history page** plots the lowest price and the **average price** per day for up to 2 years; a hover shows both for the selected date; annotation shows the % drop vs. launch/peak (e.g. "¥50,134 → ¥34,980, −30.2%"); a "日別の価格変動" table lists daily lows for 30 days and a "直近の価格変動" list shows the last 40 timestamped price registrations. [verified: https://support.kakaku.com/日々の値動きをチェックしたい-684157a10817e654ed6fe53e, https://kakaku.com/item/K0001587766/pricehistory/]
- **"Buy now vs wait."** No algorithmic verdict; the tool is positioned as "check the buy-timing from the lowest and average curves" plus a 最安お知らせメール/最安値アラート (lowest-price alert) with a target price. [verified: same support page] Pre-launch rows are labelled "参考" (reference) to separate tracked from estimated prices. [verified: pricehistory page above]
- **Offer table columns:** 価格(税込) / 送料 / 支払方法 / 在庫 / ショップ名, with shipping flagged as 送料込み (included) / 送料別 (separate) / 送料無料 (free), per-shop payment methods, stock, and the time the price was last updated; shops are ordered lowest-first and filterable by delivery region. Shipping shown is "the fee to the region nearest the shop, as a guide." [verified: https://kakaku.com/item/K0001587766/, https://help.kakaku.com/purchase.html]

## 3. SMZDM 什么值得买 (CN) — "worth it" before "where cheapest"

- **Flow:** users/editors/crawlers submit a 爆料 (deal tip) → editors (or the "小值" bot) decide whether it goes public → *all* users vote 值/不值 (worth / not worth) and comment. The vote is the primary signal, not the merchant's discount. [verified: https://sspai.com/post/92952 (2024-10-16), https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/611089696]
- **Rejecting unbelievable prices:** editors verify the promotion on the merchant site step-by-step, including stacked coupons ("需要一步一步去操作核实"); one link may be posted only once per 72h unless the price changed; a reporting mechanism rewards users for flagging fake/ad posts; order screenshots can be attached. [verified: https://my.oschina.net/elian/blog/164178, https://www.smzdm.com/p/48286639/, https://www.woshipm.com/evaluating/4468199.html]
- **Deal-post anatomy:** red current price, "xx天来最低" (lowest in N days) tag, history curve, coupon code + stacking conditions, source store, poster profile, 值/不值 counts, dispute button. [verified: https://www.woshipm.com/evaluating/4468199.html] Community caveat: the auto price curve is often inaccurate, so users rely on historical comments — a warning against trusting unreviewed history. [verified: https://sspai.com/post/92952]

## 4. Keepa / camelcamelcamel — timing from the platform's own history

- **Principle:** judge a deal from the tracker's own recorded history, never the merchant's strike-through. camelcamelcamel is described as showing history "so you can spot fake discounts before you buy." [verified, secondary: https://www.ecompath.com/articles/how-to-use-camelcamelcamel]
- **Display:** chart with Amazon / 3P-new / 3P-used lines; a stats block with **current, average, highest and lowest within the displayed window**; range switch 1m/3m/6m/1y/All; hover reveals price + date. [verified, secondary: https://www.pocket-lint.com/how-to-check-price-history-of-deals-using-camelcamelcamel/, https://clark.com/shopping-retail/how-to-use-camelcamelcamel/]
- **Keepa:** 30/90/180/365-day averages (90-day "most useful to smooth spikes"), a "Lowest ever" flag in Deals, alerts by absolute target or % drop per condition (new/used), plus availability alerts; alerts fire within minutes. [verified, secondary: https://goaura.com/blog/keepa-chrome-extension, https://fbamogul.com/keepa-product-finder-getting-started-guide/]
- **Alert UX:** "Create Price Watch" with a desired price; email when reached. [verified, secondary: pocket-lint above]
- **Failure mode to copy-avoid:** Amazon Rufus's "Price History" (30/90-day) omits prices that produced no sales, so sellers see a history that doesn't match what was listed; Amazon says individual charts can't be corrected. [verified: https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/cb8d81d0-07c1-4835-ae05-6582eaaae6bf] Lesson: show raw observations, label gaps, never silently filter.

## 5. Google Shopping (2025–2026) — parity, price insights, badges, total price

- **Landing-page parity:** price in the feed must equal the price on the landing page and in structured data; mismatch → item disapproval ("Mismatched value (page crawl) [price]"); fix = update feed, then "request a website check"; price/availability mismatch triggers *preemptive* item disapproval. [verified: https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12159029, https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13693497]
- **Total-price rule:** `price` must be "the total amount due at checkout"; all merchant-imposed fees go into `shipping`; government charges excluded; from 2025-07-01 US tax is no longer required in the feed. [verified: https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6324371, https://feedarmy.com/kb/google-updates-price-attribute-policy-in-merchant-center-april-2025/]
- **Price insights:** label "low / typical / high" based on how stores priced the item over the **past three months**, from "sellers across the web, not just Google Shopping"; a "Typical prices across the web" bar with Low/High ends and the current offer's position; "30-day low" badge is computed on **total price (item + discount + shipping)**; explicitly "does not predict future prices." [verified: https://support.google.com/faqs/answer/10675605, https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/save-money-price-insights-price-alerts/ (2025-06-25), https://9to5google.com/2020/10/22/google-shopping-price-insights/]
- **Per-retailer price history + "track price" with a budget** (notify when price ≤ what you want to spend). [verified: blog.google 2025-06-25; https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/google-shopping-ai-mode-virtual-try-on-update/ (2025-05-20)]
- **Deal badge rules:** strike-through/"sale" annotation only if the base price was charged ≥30 of the last 200 days (free listings) and the discount is >5% and <90%; "Price drop" badge is automatic when the current price is significantly below the ~60-day average and cannot be forced. [verified: https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/9017019; secondary: https://www.versafeed.com/blog/price-drop-badgeannotation-google/]

## 6. PriceRunner / PriceSpy / Pricena / Rakhys / Labeb — ranking honesty, availability, merchant conditions

- **PriceSpy rules (model text):** lowest price first, ascending; identical prices tie-break by price excl. shipping → price incl. shipping → stock → store rating → review count; "we don't prioritise profiled stores"; prices shown exclude shipping unless stated; paying shops get logo + direct link, "apart from these differences there's no distinction." [verified: https://pricespy.co.uk/info/how-we-compare-prices--i1]
- **PriceRunner:** user toggle to include/exclude delivery in total; history + alerts. [verified, secondary: https://www.cloudways.com/blog/price-comparison-websites/]
- **Pricena (sa.pricena.com/ar):** full RTL Arabic; per-offer chips before click-out: "توصيل مجاني" / "+ 12 توصيل" (delivery fee), "غير متوفر" (unavailable), "يتوفر عرض خصم" + "استخدم قسيمة الخصم" (coupon), and "السعر انخفض بنسبة 14٪". [verified: https://sa.pricena.com/ar/]
- **Rakhys (راخص):** Saudi, Arabic-only, electronics; cards show discount % + strike-through + "من N متجر"; **no** delivery, availability, freshness or history on cards; positions itself as an AI "مساعد تسوق ذكي". [verified: https://rakhys.com/about, https://www.rakhys.com/] Labeb (sa.labeb.com) is another Arabic engine. [verified: https://sa.labeb.com/]
- **"Unknown availability" handling:** no competitor shows a three-state availability; Pricena shows only "غير متوفر" when known. [unverified beyond the pages above]
- **Saudi regulatory context:** Ministry of Commerce requires a discount licence, display of price before/after, and offers the "Takhfedat/تخفيضات" barcode app to validate discounts; Maroof for store legitimacy. [verified: https://www.arabnews.com/node/1931266/page_action/aggregate (2021)] EU equivalent: prior price must be the lowest of the previous 30 days. [verified, secondary: https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/global/transparency-of-price-reductions-a-closer-look-at-the-legal-framework-in-the-eu]

## 7. AI shopping assistants (2025–2026) — needs, evidence, "we don't know", failures

- **Natural-language need → structured constraints.** Google AI Mode runs parallel sub-queries to derive criteria (e.g. "waterproof, accessible pockets") and shows a live product panel from the Shopping Graph (50B listings, "2B refreshed every hour"); "track price" takes a budget cap. [verified: https://blog.google/…/google-shopping-ai-mode-virtual-try-on-update/] ChatGPT shopping weights price first "if budget is specified"; cards carry labels ("Budget-friendly"), aggregated ratings, price + merchant links; "results are selected independently and are not ads." [verified: https://ppc.land/openai-introduces-shopping-to-chatgpt-with-1-billion-weekly-searches/, https://x.com/OpenAI/status/1916947243044856255]
- **Evidence shown:** Perplexity product cards "aren't sponsored" and are organic retrieval results with citations for specs/reviews. [verified: https://www.pymnts.com/…/perplexity-launches-ai-powered-shopping-assistant…, https://searchengineland.com/perplexity-launches-ai-powered-shopping-search-448424] Klarna (ChatGPT app, May 2026) returns "live pricing and availability from multiple retailers" via its Product Search MCP (400M listings). [verified, secondary: https://aimagazine.com/news/klarna-plugs-practical-agentic-commerce-into-chatgpt]
- **Honesty disclaimers (copy these):** OpenAI states results may not include all products, "may not be the lowest available price," price updates are delayed, taxes/delivery estimates may differ, "verify… before purchasing." [verified: ppc.land above]
- **Documented failures:** Rufus returns non-cheapest items for "cheapest," non-TVs for TV queries, invents specs/prices, recommends OOS items ("confident but not more intelligent"). [verified: https://www.marketplacepulse.com/articles/amazons-shopping-ai-is-confidently-wrong (2024-11-06), https://www.rewarx.com/blogs/amazon-rufus-hallucinated-specs-fix] Product.ai study: 4 assistants wrong/contradictory in 86% of 220 queries (97% on direct comparisons); price errors median ~$300. [verified: https://tech.co/news/ai-shopping-assistants-not-ready-holidays] Early ChatGPT checkout "pulled product info not updated in real time — prices were wrong, OOS shown as available." [verified, secondary: https://analytics-agent.app/resources/why-chatgpt-checkout-failed/] Perplexity demotes feeds with stale price/stock. [verified, secondary: https://alhena.ai/blog/perplexity-shopping-merchants-setup-guide/]
- **Mitigations in use:** merchant-pushed feeds every 15 min (OpenAI) instead of crawling; Rufus is RAG over listing text and fills gaps with category priors — so the only fix is unambiguous source facts. [verified: https://www.lengow.com/get-to-know-more/chatgpt-product-feed, rewarx above] An explicit "we don't know this spec" UI state in any assistant: **[unverified]** — no vendor documents one; the Tawveeri rule (deterministic engine decides, LLM phrases supplied facts, unknown > incorrect) is stricter than anything found.

## 8. Visual conventions for decision-grade comparison UI

- **Sparkline + annotations:** plot lowest (and optionally average) per day; label the lowest/highest observed with date; show current vs. window average/low; selectable windows (3m default); hover = price + date; mark untracked/estimated spans ("参考"). [verified: Kakaku §2, camelcamelcamel §4]
- **Price-verdict chip:** three states "low / typical / high" over a 3-month window, drawn as a range bar with the current offer placed on it; Google deliberately "leaves the decision to the buyer." [verified: §5] Deal badges only when a prior price was *actually* charged (≥30/200 days) and the drop is 5–90%. [verified: §5]
- **Total-cost breakdown:** Baymard: 64% of users look for shipping before add-to-cart; 43% of sites show no estimate; show the estimate *next to the price/CTA* with its assumption ("for Riyadh") and make it editable; show absolute and % savings together; keep all discount info adjacent to the price and state each offer once. [verified: https://baymard.com/blog/show-shipping-costs-on-product-pages, https://baymard.com/blog/product-page-price-discounts] Installation as a line item: **[unverified]** — no comparison site found that itemises it.
- **"Same model confirmed vs spec-matched":** idealo's EAN-matched vs "free catalog" split and Google's GTIN dependence are the industry precedent for a two-tier identity badge. [verified: §1, §5] No consumer-facing badge wording found. [unverified]
- **Budget-cap compliance:** Google and ChatGPT both accept a budget and label/filter on it. [verified: §7]
- **Freshness stamp:** Kakaku shows per-offer "last updated" time; Google advertises hourly refresh; AI engines treat stale price as a demotion signal. [verified: §2, §5, §7]
- **Comparison tables (NN/g, 2024-02-09):** ≤5 items (dynamic 3–4), products as columns, attributes as rows, fixed headers on scroll, a "highlight differences" toggle, ≤2 columns on mobile, short values not sentences, "do the work for the consumer." [verified: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/comparison-tables/]
- **Arabic/RTL specifics:** no published RTL comparison-UX research found; Pricena is the only live RTL reference. [unverified]
