## What was deliberately NOT included
- Ramadan/Eid: the next Ramadan begins in February 2027, outside this 90-day window — do not create Ramadan content against this calendar.
- University/school-year start: already passed before this pack's 2026-09-06 baseline (typically late August/early September in Saudi Arabia) — not a forward-looking opportunity in this window.
- Any date requiring a specific merchant's own promotional calendar (e.g. "Jarir's National Day sale runs X to Y") — this pack has no live evidence of specific 2026 merchant promotional dates; verify before naming a merchant's specific sale window.

## Standing rule
Every entry above that reaches GREEN does so because it maps to an already-evidenced Tawveeri capability (budget parsing, fake-discount detection) — the calendar does not invent new capabilities for a seasonal moment. If a season suggests new content that requires an UNKNOWN capability (e.g., "gift bundling for National Day"), do not promise it; either skip that angle or flag it as a Product Gap for engineering to consider.
