App Requirements :   
 
Project Overview  
The platform is a web -based solution that enables customers in Saudi Arabia to compare 
prices of electronic devices (e.g., laptops, TVs, smartphones) across multiple local and 
online electronic stores (e.g., Extra, Almanae, Jarir). Customers can search for a speciCic 
product, Cilter by brand, model, and speciCications, and the platform will return results 
with prices from different stores. Customers can then be redirected to the store with the 
lowest or preferred price. Phase 1 focuses on growth and adoption, while Phase 2 
introduces proCitability through commissions from sales.  
  
Functional Requirements  
• User Registration & Authentication: Allow optional account creation via email, 
phone, or social login.  
• Guest Access: Customers can search without creating an account.  
• Product Search: Search by product category (e.g., TV, Laptop, Smartphone).  
• Brand & Model Filtering: Narrow down results by brand, model, speciCications.  
• Advanced Filtering: By size, RAM, storage, resolution, color, etc.  
• Price Comparison Engine: Compare product prices across integrated stores.  
• Sort & Filter Results: Sort by lowest price, popularity, rating, or store name.  
• Store Integration: APIs/web scraping to fetch real -time prices from stores like 
Extra, Jarir, Almanea.  
• Redirection to Store: Click -through to redirect customer to the store website with 
selected product.  
• Product Detail Page: Display speciCications, images, and available stores with 
pricing.  
• Wishlist/Save Products: Users can save products for later viewing.

Non-Functional Requirements  
• Performance: Search results should load in under 3 seconds.  
• Scalability: Ability to handle 1 million+ users and concurrent searches.  
• Security: Protect user data with SSL, encryption, and secure login methods.  
• Reliability: 99.9% uptime with automated monitoring.  
• Data Accuracy: Ensure store data is refreshed regularly and reliably.  
• Usability: Simple and intuitive UI for both Arabic and English users.  
• SEO Optimization: Ensure discoverability on search engines.  
• Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 compliance for users with disabilities.  
• Maintainability: Modular codebase for easy updates and new store integrations.  
• Legal Compliance: Align with Saudi e -commerce and data privacy laws.  
• Cross -Browser Compatibility: Support Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox.  
• Mobile Optimization: Smooth experience across iOS and Android devices.  
• Data Backup: Daily automated backups of product data and user preferences.  
• Monitoring & Logging: Full logging of API requests and error tracking.  
• Energy EfCiciency: Optimized infrastructure to reduce hosting costs.  
• Localization: Support Hijri and Gregorian dates, Arabic numerals.
