## SLIDE 2: Problem Statement

**Title:** The Challenge

**Content:**
- Consumers in Saudi Arabia struggle to find the best prices for electronics
- Multiple stores (Extra, Jarir, Noon, Amazon.sa, Almanea) with different prices
- No centralized platform to compare prices across stores
- Price tracking and alerts are manual and time-consuming
- Language barrier: Need for Arabic-first experience
- Lack of trust: No reviews or ratings for stores

**Key Points:**
- Time-consuming price research
- Missed deals and price drops
- Language and UX barriers
- No price history tracking
